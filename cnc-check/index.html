Drawing (blueprint)

Take a photo of the print, or pick the drawing as an image or PDF. I read the sizes, the stock note, holes and units off it. Optional, but it helps.

📄 Photo or file of the drawing

Blank, from the top

Lay the blank flat in the middle of a sheet of plain white letter paper on a darker table. Get the whole sheet in the photo and shoot from about 1½ ft above. I find the paper and the blank and measure it.
📷 Top photo

The outlines are draggable if one is off.

Height (side photo)

Tip the blank onto its side on the same paper and take one more photo from above. I read the height off it. Skip it and I use the drawing or the program.
📷 Side photo
No paper? Measure with a ruler instead

Lay a ruler or 1-2-3 block next to the blank, take a photo, tap both ends of the ruler, then the blank's corners (or 3 points on a round edge).

📷 Photo with ruler

Drag to turn, pinch to zoom. Tap a problem to see where it happens.

    This checks the blank, the jaws or chuck, and the table under the part against the tool tip and diameter. It doesn't model tool holders, the spindle nose, or fixtures it can't see.
